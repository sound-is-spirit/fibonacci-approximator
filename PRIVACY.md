# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

## Overview

The "Fibonacci Approximator" Chrome Extension respects your privacy. This policy outlines exactly what happens with your data when you use the extension.

## Data Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or browsing history.**

- **No Analytics:** We do not use any analytics software or tracking scripts (e.g., Google Analytics).
- **No External Servers:** The extension does not communicate with any external servers. All mathematical calculations happen 100% locally on your machine within your browser.
- **In-Memory Execution:** The integer you input into the calculator is only held in your browser's temporary memory while the overlay is open and is immediately discarded when you close the extension.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions for functional purposes only:
- **`activeTab`**: Used temporarily when you click the extension icon to verify the URL before injecting the user interface, preventing execution on restricted browser pages.
- **`scripting`**: Used strictly to inject the calculator's user interface overlay into your current webpage.

These permissions are never used to monitor your browsing behavior or extract information from the pages you visit.
